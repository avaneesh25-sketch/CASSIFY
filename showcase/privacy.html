Privacy choices

Updated 28 October 2026. This notice covers the Cassify showcase hosted at casify-two.vercel.app.

The current prototype

Google sign-in is optional. When you sign in, Google shares your basic profile and email with Supabase Authentication to create your Cassify account. We save your selected answers to the three sample cases against your account ID so you can return on another device. Other students cannot read your saved answers. Session credentials are stored in your browser to keep you signed in; use Sign out on shared devices. Practice XP is calculated from your saved sample cases. The campus leaderboard remains illustrative. For account or data-deletion requests, contact avaneeshpramod25@gmail.com. Hosting providers may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Optional Microsoft Clarity analytics

If you select Allow analytics, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our public landing page, including interactions, heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity can process device and browser information and uses cookies to associate visits. We mask case-dialog content, do not send case answers or email addresses as custom events, and keep advertising-storage consent denied.

Clarity is not loaded until you allow analytics. Declining analytics does not prevent you from using the showcase. No Clarity script is loaded on this privacy page. Read Microsoft’s privacy statement for information about Microsoft’s processing.

Change your choice

Use Privacy choices below or in the page footer to allow or decline analytics. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If you withdraw permission on the landing page, it reloads to stop the running analytics script and prevent it from loading again. Withdrawing permission does not remove previously collected data.

Search performance and payments

Google Search Console helps the site owner understand Google Search performance; its verification tag does not install visitor analytics. Live payments and premium access are not active on this showcase. A clearly labelled Dodo test checkout is available at /billing-test.html. If you choose to enter information there, Dodo processes it under its own privacy policy. Do not enter real payment-card details. No Clarity analytics runs on the test checkout page. Future live payment features will need an updated privacy notice before launch.
